Grocery List

About Grocery List

Grocery List is a lightweight shopping companion designed to streamline your errands from planning to checkout. Avoid overspending and forgotten ingredients by organizing your purchases and tracking actual costs in one place.

Core Features

  • Interactive Planner: Add grocery items with custom quantities to keep your trip focused.
  • Live Price Tracking: Record item prices on the spot in ₱ (PHP) and transfer them directly to your checkout list.
  • Budget Summary: Keep an eye on running totals for both planned item quantities and total cart costs .
  • Accident-Proof: Built-in confirmation dialogs prevent accidental list deletions.

How to Use

  1. Add your target grocery items and quantities under the Planner tab.
  2. In the store, click Buy Item on any item to log its price and move it to your cart.
  3. Check the Checkout tab to verify your purchases and monitor your total bill.
